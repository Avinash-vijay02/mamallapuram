10 stops to explore

A sample one-day trail

  1. Sunrise · Shore Temple & beachWatch the sun come up behind the temple, then walk the sand past the fishing boats.
  2. Morning · Pancha RathasSee the five stone chariots and the carved elephant before it gets hot.
  3. Late morning · The granite hillArjuna's Penance, Krishna's Butter Ball and the cave temples are all a short walk apart.
  4. Lunch · Local seafoodTry a fresh catch at one of the small eateries near the beach.
  5. Afternoon · Lighthouse & sculptors' streetClimb for the view, then watch stone carvers at work.
  6. Sunset · Tiger CaveDrive a few kilometres north for a quiet seaside finish.
